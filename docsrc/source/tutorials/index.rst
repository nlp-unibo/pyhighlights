Tutorials
=========

Two tutorials, in order.
:doc:`quickstart` runs one of the library's architectures end to end and reads the results back, and :doc:`custom-model` writes a method of your own and runs it through the same task.

- :doc:`quickstart` needs nothing but the package, and finishes in seconds on a synthetic corpus.
- :doc:`custom-model` is what to do once one of the eight architectures is not the experiment you want: a new criterion, a new architecture, and the registrations that make both a key.
